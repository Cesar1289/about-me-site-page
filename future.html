Where I'm Headed: The Next 5 Years

My roadmap from business student to business professional — the milestones I am working toward, and the life I want to build along the way.

My 5-Year Roadmap

Years 1–2

Finish Strong Academically

My first priority is to complete my core business coursework with strong grades, especially in marketing, finance, and management. I also want to join a business club on campus to practice presentation and teamwork skills outside the classroom.

Year 3

Real-World Experience

I plan to complete at least one internship in marketing or sales. Getting hands-on experience at a real company is the fastest way for me to learn how the ideas from my classes actually work in practice — and to start building a professional network.

Year 4

Graduate and Start My Career

I want to graduate with my business degree and land my first full-time role as a marketing or sales associate. My goal is to join a company with a strong training culture where I can keep learning every day.

Year 5

Grow, Lead, and Launch

By year five I want to have earned a promotion based on results, and to have launched my own small e-commerce side business to apply everything I have learned about marketing, customers, and operations firsthand.

🎯 My Long-Term Goal

Within five years, I will graduate with my business degree, gain two years of professional marketing experience through an internship and a full-time role, and launch my own small online business with at least 100 customers.

Beyond the Roadmap

Build Something of My Own

Entrepreneurship is my dream. Every business class I take is a step toward running a company that creates jobs and serves customers well.

Never Stop Learning

Certifications, mentors, books, and real projects — I want to keep improving my skills long after graduation day.
